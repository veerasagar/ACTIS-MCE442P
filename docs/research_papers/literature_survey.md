# Literature Survey for ACTIS / PrivaSyn

This document presents a comprehensive literature survey in a tabular format, synthesizing the methodologies of the 38 gathered research papers and their specific relevance to the **Agentic Collaborative Threat Intelligence System (ACTIS)**.

| Ref # | Authors & Title | Year | Core Methodology & Contribution | Relevance to ACTIS / PrivaSyn |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Zhang, Yang, et al.**<br>"Tri-LLM Cooperative Federated Zero-Shot Intrusion Detection..." | 2026 | Proposes a federated zero-shot IDS using semantic disagreement across tri-LLMs and Trust-Aware aggregation. | **Primary Base:** Foundational for the Trust-Aware FedProx multi-node training and handling semantic drift. |
| **2** | **Chen, Liu, et al.**<br> "ReGAIN: Retrieval-Grounded AI Framework for Network Traffic Analysis" | 2025 | Introduces a RAG-based framework for interpretable network traffic analysis and citation-backed reasoning. | **Primary Base:** Core architecture for the Global RAG Knowledge Base and telemetry vectorization. |
| **3** | **Yuan, Zheng, et al.**<br> "CyberRAG: Agentic RAG Cyber Attack Classification and Reporting Tool" | 2025 | Develops an agent-driven architecture with modular classifiers, iterative retrieval, and structured reporting. | **Primary Base:** Inspirations for the Agentic Orchestration engine, report synthesis, and interactive chat. |
| 4 | **Tariq et al.**<br> "Federated Learning based Network Intrusion Detection using Unbalanced IoT Data" | 2024 | Applies FL to counter severe class imbalances specifically in IoT network topologies. | Addresses localized extreme non-IID class imbalance prior to FedProx aggregation. |
| 5 | **Wu, Lee, et al.**<br> "Large Language Models in Cybersecurity: A Systematic Review" | 2024 | Thorough review of LLM capabilities, limits, and applications within defensive cyber operations. | Contextualizes the choice of Gemini 2.0 Flash for structured threat extraction. |
| 6 | **Kim & Cho**<br> "Agent-Based Autonomous Threat Landscape Analysis using LLMs" | 2024 | Demonstrates multi-agent topologies mapping active threats to defensive posture autonomously. | Supports the Agentic Red-Teaming loop proposed in the improvement plan. |
| 7 | **Huang et al.**<br> "Evaluating the Privacy of Large Language Model Ecosystems" | 2024 | Quantifies data extraction vulnerabilities and memorization risks within foundational LLMs. | Highlights the critical need for strict Regex + LLM combined PII sanitization. |
| 8 | **Wang et al.**<br> "Empowering Zero-Day Attack Discovery using Context-Aware GPTs" | 2024 | Explores using pre-trained generative transformers for matching anomalous payload signatures. | Supplements the zero-day classifier thresholds. |
| 9 | **Lin et al.**<br> "PII Sanitization in Large Language Models: Methodologies, Datasets..." | 2024 | Studies automated redaction techniques ensuring zero-leakage without losing semantic context. | Foundational for the ACTIS "Agent Sanitizer" pipeline. |
| 10 | **Hu et al.**<br> "RAG-Sec: Enhancing Zero-Day Vulnerability Analysis using RAG" | 2024 | Integrates real-time external intelligence feeds to enrich isolated vulnerability analyses. | Maps to how ACTIS queries CVEs and MITRE TTPs via ChromaDB. |
| 11 | **Zhang, Yang, et al.**<br> "Synergizing Federated Learning and Vector Databases for Cyber Immunity..." | 2024 | Links distributed ML models with decentralized vector storage for proactive defense. | Direct precursor mapping to the ACTIS "Immunity Engine." |
| 12 | **Chen et al.**<br> "A Federated Learning Approach to Network Intrusion Detection Systems" | 2023 | Establishes a baseline methodology for federating traditional IDS architectures securely. | Core benchmark for the localized MLP IDS implementations. |
| 13 | **Reynaud, Gupta, et al.**<br> "Collaborative and Privacy-Preserving Intrusion Detection in Edge Computing..." | 2023 | Focuses on shifting FL computational burdens to Edge/Fog nodes without compromising privacy. | Validates deploying lightweight PyTorch MLPs directly at the network edge. |
| 14 | **Liu et al.**<br> "A Scalable and Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning Framework for Securing IoT..." | 2023 | Scales federated topologies specifically optimized for low-compute endpoints. | Confirms feasibility of federating across constrained smart environments. |
| 15 | **Zhao et al.**<br> "Federated Learning with Non-IID Data: A Survey" | 2023 | Comprehensive taxonomization of mechanisms (like FedProx) to solve statistical non-IID shifts. | Justifies using FedProx proximal term against standard FedAvg. |
| 16 | **Huang, Zhu, et al.**<br> "Dynamic Trust-Aware Federated Learning in Unreliable Environments" | 2023 | Proposes dynamic reliability scores for FL participants based on historical loss drops. | Theoretical backing for the dynamic client penalization ($1/L_i$). |
| 17 | **Wang, Huang, & Dai**<br> "Differentially Private Vector Embeddings for Secure Information Retrieval" | 2023 | Formalizes injecting $\cal{N}$ noise into semantic vector spaces to prevent exact string reversal. | Core math behind the ACTIS mathematical DP layer ($\epsilon, \delta$). |
| 18 | **Badi et al.**<br> "Quantifying and Mitigating Data Leakage in Vector Embeddings via Differential Privacy" | 2023 | Demonstrates how membership inference works against dense vectors and how DP mitigates it. | Threat model validation for protecting the global threat reports. |
| 19 | **Chen et al.**<br> "Local Differential Privacy for Deep Learning and Edge Computing Architectures" | 2023 | Explores Local DP bounds compared to central DP mechanisms during active federated training. | Options for expanding DP to the local PyTorch parameter gradients. |
| 20 | **Qiao et al.**<br> "Generative Red Teaming: Integrating LLMs for Automated Penetration Testing..." | 2023 | Employs GenAI to probabilistically discover flaws in system defense profiles. | Relates to the Adversarial / Validation Sub-Agent for generated firewall rules. |
| 21 | **Yuan, Zheng, et al.**<br> "Agentic AI Frameworks for Automated Cyber Defense Mechanisms" | 2023 | Discusses bridging logical AI reasoning directly into operational infrastructure constraints. | Validates the "actionable defense" translation into `iptables` rules. |
| 22 | **Chen, Liu, et al.**<br> "Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Dynamic Knowledge Graphs in CTI" | 2023 | Enhances standard dense-vector RAG by overlaying linked semantic knowledge pathways. | Inspiration for upgrading ChromaDB to include GraphRAG KV-Stores. |
| 23 | **Vasylenko et al.**<br> "Automating Defense Infrastructure (Immunity) via Threat Pattern Retrieval" | 2023 | Proves semantic similarity searches can effectively identify identical lateral attack movements. | Forms the core premise behind the ACTIS "Immunity" mechanism. |
| 24 | **Benchea et al.**<br> "Applying Semantic Vector Databases for Threat Actor Profiling..." | 2023 | Tracks distinct persistent threat entities entirely through multi-dimensional vector profiling. | Guides exactly how the telemetry/heuristic databases should be partitioned. |
| 25 | **Mothukuri et al.**<br> "Federated-Learning-Based Anomaly Detection for IoT Security" | 2022 | Early baseline for distributed anomaly detection leveraging FL primitives. | Baseline comparative reference for the NF-BoT-IoT-v2 dataset usage. |
| 26 | **Zhang, Chen, et al.**<br> "Federated Learning for Industrial Internet of Things: Framework..." | 2022 | Reviews strict synchronization and latency constraints when federating SCADA/IIoT networks. | Guides the architectural constraints for near-real-time updates. |
| 27 | **Alassery et al.**<br> "An Intelligent Mechanism for Enhancing Intrusion Detection using Federated Deep Learning" | 2022 | Showcases deep architectures (MLP, CNN) inside FL loops outperforming local models. | Validates the 128-64-32 global topology. |
| 28 | **Zhang et al.**<br> "Trust-Aware Federated Learning Framework for Reliable Distributed Systems" | 2022 | Identifies Sybil and poisoning threats in FL, proposing behavioral trust bounding. | Outlines the exact attack vectors Trust-Aware aggregation halts. |
| 29 | **Yin et al.**<br> "FedRobust: Robust Federated Learning with Byzantine Nodes" | 2022 | Implements Byzantine-resilient bounding logic (K-RUM/Medians) for malicious updates. | Improvement plan core inclusion for Sybil resistance. |
| 30 | **Wu et al.**<br> "Defending against Malicious Clients in Federated Learning via Robust Aggregation" | 2022 | Discusses mathematical bounds on how many corrupted clients an aggregation can survive. | Theoretical limits of the current FL server. |
| 31 | **Lyu, Yu, et al.**<br> "Privacy and Robustness in Federated Learning: Attacks and Defenses" | 2022 | Holistic review of both inversion risks and poisoning, proposing bounded mitigations. | The core threat-model framework for ACTIS. |
| 32 | **Li et al.**<br> "DeepFed: Federated Deep Learning for Intrusion Detection in Industrial CPS" | 2021 | Demonstrates practical application of Deep FL on large-scale physical telemetry. | Baseline for mapping network features to tensor blocks. |
| 33 | **Fung, Yoon, & Beschastnikh**<br> "Mitigating Sybils in Federated Learning Poisoning" | 2021 | Details specifically how Sybil attacks emulate honest profiles before poisoning bounds. | Important reference for adversarial testing protocols. |
| 34 | **Hao, Li, et al.**<br> "Efficient and Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning with Differential Privacy" | 2021 | Evaluates the trade-off strictly between utility (accuracy) and privacy ($\epsilon$ bounds) in FL. | Benchmark for balancing the Differential Privacy multiplier $\sigma$. |
| 35 | **Truex et al.**<br> "A Hybrid Approach to Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning" | 2021 | Argues for combining MPC (Multi-Party Compute) alongside DP for maximum security. | Potential layer for secure computation among untrusted nodes. |
| 36 | **Li, Sahu, et al.**<br> "Federated Optimization in Heterogeneous Networks (FedProx)" | 2020 | **Seminal Paper:** Formally defines the FedProx proximal $\mu$-term regularization algorithm. | Core underpinning mechanism ensuring ACTIS convergence on non-IID traffic. |
| 37 | **Fallah, Mokhtari, & Ozdaglar**<br> "Personalized Federated Learning with Theoretical Guarantees..." | 2020 | **Seminal Paper:** Proposes mathematical bounds for Personalized FL (pFL) via Meta-Learning. | Foundational logic behind retaining localized untethered linear heads. |
| 38 | **Wei et al.**<br> "Federated Learning with Differential Privacy: Algorithms and Performance Analysis" | 2020 | **Seminal Paper:** Formally establishes how injected parametric noise limits inversion leakage in federated networks. | Core theorem backing for the mathematical DP guarantees. |
